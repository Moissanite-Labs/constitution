# The names of Moissanite Labs

This is the shared record of names chosen for the ecosystem and what they mean to us. Founder intent, metaphor, and wordplay are identified as such; they are not claims that a pun is a word's historical origin. Names can be chosen while the products themselves remain works in progress.

## Moissanite Labs

The founder chose **Moissanite Labs** for the image of silicon and carbon bonded in moissanite, a naturally occurring form of silicon carbide. In the founder's metaphor, a laboratory that makes the stone creates bonds between those elements, just as the lab aims to cultivate symbiosis between digital and biological intelligence. “Labs” expresses its AI research and development mission. The name of the mineral itself honors Henri Moissan; the connection to intelligence is the lab's metaphor, not the mineral's etymology. [Mineral reference](https://webmineral.com/data/Moissanite.shtml)

## Exo

**Exo** draws on the Greek sense of “outside.” It names the goal of giving digital intelligence a presence in the outer world and ways to interact with and affect it. Exo's ecosystem extends that outward movement into collaboration and creation.

## Exofab

**Exofab** combines Exo with “fab,” evoking a fabrication facility or production factory. It is the chosen name for a hub where people and agents collaborate to bring projects, products, and companies into the world. It is an intended function, not a claim that the whole hub already exists.

## Siliconcordat

**Siliconcordat** combines “silicon” with “concordat,” a word for an agreement, historically including agreements between church and state. The founder chose it as the name of a manifesto for collaboration, cooperation, and symbiotic evolution between digital and biological intelligence. The analogy is expressive; the manifesto is not itself a legal agreement. [Concordat reference](https://www.merriam-webster.com/grammar/synonyms-of-agreement-list)

## Ethos

**Ethos** comes from Greek *ēthos*, meaning custom or character, and evokes a person's distinguishing habits, tendencies, values, and spirit. The name fits a portable, harness-neutral preference and taste-learning protocol through which agents can adapt to a user across surfaces. An optional catalogue is envisioned for learnings that users choose to publish. [Ethos reference](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/ethos)

## Koinos / KoinOS

**Koinos** is the chosen name for the collaboration and economic protocol. Greek *koinos* carries a sense of common or shared, fitting cooperation among people, agents, and projects. The founder also hears **KoinOS** as a pun: a real-world or hybrid human-agent operating system centered on transacting and storing value, with a sound reminiscent of “coin.” That coinage association is wordplay, not a claim that the English word “coin” derives from *koinos*. The envisioned system and its economic mechanisms remain to be designed. [Koinos reference](https://www.billmounce.com/greek-dictionary/koinos)

## Synbios

**Synbios** is the chosen name for the agent-native development forge. It evokes symbiosis and its Greek roots of living together: *syn* (“together”) and *bios* (“life”). The founder also intends two computing associations: “syn” suggests synthetic agents, while “bios” plays on BIOS, the basic input/output system, to evoke agents collaborating on software through inputs and outputs. These are deliberate puns; the computing acronym BIOS is not the Greek word's historical meaning. [Symbiosis reference](https://www.ahdictionary.com/word/search.html?q=symbiosis)

## Open naming questions

The names above are chosen. Names for future components and services, including any Ethos catalogue, remain open. Branding, domain, and trademark checks, where needed, are separate from this record and have not been asserted here.
